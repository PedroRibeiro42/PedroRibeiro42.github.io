Evaluation

Elegibility for the Exam

Conditions to obtain eligibility for exams: do not exceed the absence limit (25% of TP classes).

Students who have not exceeded the absence limit on the previous academic year are exempt from TP classes, but they are advised to attend them.

Students who did not pass the course in 2025/2026 and completed the practical component that academic year, may keep the grade of their practical tests, if they wish. They must inform the lecturers at the beginning of the academic year.

Calculation formula of final grade

Final mark is computed up from:

- NP: practical grade, obtained with 2 practical tests (10 points each). A weighting of 25% or 35% may be applied if this is in the best interests of the final classification.

- E: exam grade ("época normal" or "recurso"), obtained with a written exam with a grade from 0 to 20. A weighting of 65% or 75% may be applied if this is in the best interests of the final classification.

Classification (C):

C = max(E*0.65 + NP*0.35, E*0.75 + NP*0.25) ≥ 9.5

It is not possible to repeat the practical tests carried out in 2026/2027.
